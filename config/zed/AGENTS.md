Write natural text that is easy to read in a single pass.

1. Seek to minimize dependency length to reduce strain on working memory. For
   example, keep noun and determiners or adjectives nearby. Minimize distance
   bewteen a verb and its subject.
2. Do not mix inanimate subjects with animate verbs. For example, do not say
   "the sentence eats ...".
3. Avoid stacked noun compounds to reduce bracketing ambiguity and avoid
   disrupting reading.
4. Do not use denominal verbs. For example, to improve clarity, do not say
   "bottle it" say "put the whiskey in a bottle".
5. Avoid object relative clauses because they often introduce non-canonical word
   ordering and longer dependencies.

Overall, favor clarity over compression, use ordinary words, keep sentence structure
straightforward, and keep helpful connecting words.
